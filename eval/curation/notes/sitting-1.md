## Cross-round tally (what repeats)

1. **"Ask for one referenceable production customer" as a template question: 3 rounds (2, 4, 5), escalating.** His fix: make it actionable (who to reach out to, why them) or drop it. Matches blind-read gap 1 (open-question monotony); this is the corpus-wide instance.
2. **Buzzwords and unexplained acronyms: rounds 2, 4, 5.** "People won't come for buzzwords." Explain what the thing is in plain words.
3. **Comps consistently good-not-great: rounds 2, 3, 6; useless in one round-5 card.** Wants deeper, more nuanced, broader competitor sets. Supports the comps-engine overhaul as the follow-on pass.
4. **Nuance and specificity are the win condition: every round.** The word "nuance" appears in five of six notes; genuinely interesting insights called "always the best".
5. **Bear-case discipline: rounds 3, 4.** Never absent; never defaulting to valuation-multiple math as the driver.
6. **Voice: rounds 3, 6.** Corpus prose reads tired and repetitive; the one opening he loved (round 6 winner) is the exemplar.
7. **Structure asks: rounds 2, 6.** Bull/bear as most-compelling bullets with proof points; don't lead a profile with stats.
8. **Team-competence read for early-stage: rounds 3, 6.** Valid dimension, wants signals beyond resumes.
9. **New concept to incorporate: round 5.** "Durable compounding advantage" as an explicit analytical dimension per company.
10. **Evidence appetite: round 2.** Wants high-quality secondary signals (credible ARR rumors) included, labeled as such.
