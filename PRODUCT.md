# Cold Start

## Register

product

## Users

Cold Start is for investors and company researchers doing a first serious read before a call, memo, partner conversation, or screening decision. They are usually working in a narrow browser side panel with limited attention. They need the public facts quickly, then a concise point of view that helps them decide what matters and what to ask next.

## Product Purpose

Cold Start turns scattered public company evidence into a sourced profile and a sharp investor read. The profile establishes facts and provenance. Investor Lens should then surface the most useful supported interpretation: why the company could matter, what must hold, what could break, why timing matters, and which question would change conviction. Success means the reader understands the business and has a better next question within minutes, without confusing confidence for certainty.

## Brand Personality

Clear, discerning, and alive. Cold Start should feel authored by an investor with strong judgment and product taste, not generated by a model or assembled from database fields. The interface is calm enough to trust, direct enough to scan, and enjoyable enough to invite exploration.

## Anti-references

- Parchment dossiers, beige editorial templates, faux filing-room decoration, and other AI-generated research aesthetics.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, chat interfaces, scoring systems, and large colored status treatments.
- Walls of memo prose, oversized display copy in narrow columns, tiny low-contrast metadata, and decorative hierarchy that competes with the evidence.
- Internal pipeline language such as verification, schemas, models, claims surviving, or evidence gates on ordinary user-facing surfaces.
- Confident unsupported conclusions, generic diligence checklists, and polished filler that could describe any company.

## Design Principles

- Facts establish trust; judgment earns attention. Keep the two distinct without making the reader operate the pipeline.
- Lead with the load-bearing point. Every Lens section should say one useful thing before offering depth.
- Make uncertainty useful. When evidence is thin, say what is known, what is missing, and what would change the read in plain investor language.
- Progressive disclosure should reduce cognitive load, not hide broken formatting or move a wall of text behind another click.
- Distinctive interaction comes from precise hierarchy, tactile response, and the Cold Start aperture motif. It does not come from ornamental paper, invented stamps, or extra chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and complete keyboard operation. Core meaning must never depend on color, hover, animation, or fine pointer input. Respect reduced motion, preserve visible focus, keep touch targets at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, support browser text zoom, and maintain readable type at Chrome side-panel widths.
