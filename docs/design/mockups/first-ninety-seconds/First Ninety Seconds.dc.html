The seal at five stages
filed
Dashed before consent. One arc per closed stage. On the fifth it fills and takes the stamp. It is the only thing in the panel that moves on its own.
The wit
A clipping does not vanish. It dims and slides into the rule that counts what is already read, so the pile of read paper stays visible. By the end that line and the seal agree.
Held to
Source class shows as a mark, never a tint. Absence keeps its own mark. No stage is claimed before its event lands, and no number appears that the pipeline cannot prove.
