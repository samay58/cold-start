# How it wins judgment standard

Status: authoritative for Cold Start's mapping of company evidence to Steph Ango's 80-strategy Moats framework.

This document governs judgment before prose. Steph's names and meanings remain canonical. The [strategy rubric](./2026-08-21-how-it-wins-strategy-rubric.md) gives Cold Start's operational tests. Those tests help apply the framework; they do not redefine it.

## Find the company's actual bet

Ask what the company's actual bet is. In one worked example, cloud agents appeared in the evidence nine times and neither writer centred them.

Identify the bet before applying strategy labels. A candidate bet is the mechanism that best explains the company's consequential product, customer, economic, and organizational choices. Test it for:

- coverage of the company's major choices
- costly, persistent, or constraining commitments
- consequences if the bet fails
- evidence that it remains part of the company's direction
- survival after the most vivid fact is removed from the evidence

A financing, lawsuit, acquisition, failure, customer name, or announcement is not central merely because it is dramatic.

Split a company into multiple strategic bets only when each proposed bet has an independently evidenced win mechanism and consequential commitments, and combining them would conceal a material difference in customer decision, economics, competitive set, operating model, or product architecture. Product count, geography, or organizational structure alone is not enough.

Record the company-level bet, any material sub-bets, the alternatives considered, and why the chosen scope does not conceal or manufacture a difference.

## Keep claims separate

Every claim in the audit has one type.

| Claim type | Meaning | Required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed fact | A statement reported, measured, demonstrated, or recorded by a source without added causal meaning. | Cite its evidence ID. Preserve attribution, scope, date, and uncertainty. A company claim remains a company claim. |
| Reasonable inference | A conclusion supported by observed facts through a short, explicit bridge. | Cite every supporting fact and state the bridge. Record meaningful counterevidence. |
| Judgment | An application of this standard, including the bet, materiality, sibling choice, strategy disposition, ordering, or pair. | Link it to the facts and inferences on which it rests. Do not present it as sourced fact. |
| Open question | A specific unresolved fact that could materially change a bet, strategy, pair, or not-yet decision. | State why it matters, what evidence would answer it, and which judgment could change. |
| Insufficient evidence | The record cannot establish or disqualify a mechanism. | Treat it as a disposition about the record, never as a claim about the company. |
| Unsupported speculation | A proposed mechanism with no observed precursor, an invented causal step, or a hidden premise. | Exclude it from current and not yet. |

Absence of data is never analysis.

Missing evidence is not disqualifying evidence. Disqualification requires affirmative evidence against the mechanism. A citation does not strengthen a claim beyond what the source supports.

## Evaluate all 80 strategies

Evaluate every canonical strategy against each material company bet. Store one disposition for every strategy. Use the exact canonical label and meaning.

Do not put a numerical cap on the current strategy set. That is artificial and makes no logical sense.

The selected set can contain zero, one, or several strategies. A display budget may collapse supported strategies in the interface. It may not change the stored judgment, relabel a supported strategy, or convert one supported strategy into nothing stands out.

## Require a positive case for current

A current strategy must pass every applicable gate:

- Evidence shows that the canonical mechanism operates.
- The mechanism materially affects buyer choice, economics, competitive conduct, or the operating model.
- The mechanism does strategic work in the present.
- The nearest plausible siblings have been resolved from evidence.
- The mechanism is not a duplicate of another selected strategy.
- Removing the strategy would make the account of the company's bet meaningfully less accurate.

A capability may be real and still fail selection because it is peripheral, generic, duplicated, historical only, or immaterial.

Prestige needs the brand to change a buyer's decision. A top-tier cap table is not Prestige.

Iteration needs a feedback loop, not a release count or a raise.

## Record judgment dimensions without a score

Record evidence strength, centrality, materiality, distinctiveness, and independence as separate categorical judgments for every strategy with positive support; strategies that fail the evidence gate may mark the remaining fields “not reached.” Do not combine the dimensions into a score. Evidence, materiality, independence, and explanatory value are current-selection gates, while centrality and distinctiveness govern ordering and interpretation.

Use these questions:

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence strength | How directly and comprehensively is the mechanism supported? |
| Centrality | How much does it explain the company's actual bet and consequential choices? |
| Materiality | Does it change buyer choice, economics, competitive conduct, or operations? |
| Distinctiveness | Is it specific to this company or merely a category baseline? |
| Independence | Does it add a causal mechanism not already captured by another selected strategy? |

Order current strategies primarily by centrality. Use evidence strength, materiality, and distinctiveness to explain close calls. No strong dimension can compensate for a failed selection gate.

## Resolve sibling labels

Sibling labels such as Alliance and Symbiosis, or Usership and Reliability, need a stated reason to differ.

For every positively supported strategy with a plausible sibling, record:

- the shared evidence or ambiguity
- the rubric's deciding question
- the evidence-backed answer
- why the rejected sibling would misdescribe the mechanism
- when both survive, the distinct causal role of each

“X fits better than Y” is not a reason. The answer must name a discriminating fact. The same evidence may support two labels only when it performs two distinct causal roles.

## Treat history as evidence for the present

A historical mechanism counts as current only when recent evidence shows that it still operates, or when a present material outcome is demonstrably dependent on the accumulated historical asset. Historical existence alone is insufficient.

Older evidence can establish origin, persistence, and causality. It cannot establish current strategy without a bridge to a present outcome.

## Keep not yet near and testable

A not-yet strategy needs a named condition that could plausibly make the mechanism current within 12 to 24 months. A longer-horizon possibility is an open question, not a not-yet strategy, even when the sector moves slowly.

A not-yet strategy also needs:

- an observed precursor today
- a causal path from precursor to mechanism
- the reason the mechanism is not current
- the evidence that would promote it to current

Without those elements, reject it as unsupported speculation or retain the specific uncertainty as an open question.

## Make an unusual pair earn the claim

A pair has to exclude something before it counts as unusual.

A pair is unusual only when evidence shows that comparable competitors normally make a different choice, and that combining these two strategies excludes or constrains that choice. If we cannot establish the competitors or their normal choice, do not claim the pair is unusual.

Treat the pair as a secondary, optional judgment. It does not change which strategies are current, their order, or the account of the company's actual bet. Failure to establish a pair does not weaken an otherwise supported strategy mapping.

Cold Start's operational test requires:

- two independently current strategies
- a sourced reference class of credible substitutes competing for the same buyer decision
- affirmative evidence of the concrete choice those competitors normally make
- the alternative that the pair excludes or constrains
- evidence that the company accepts the resulting cost or commitment
- a causal interaction between the two strategies, not mere co-occurrence
- the linked changes a competitor would need to copy both

Absence of observed competitors using the pair is not evidence of unusualness. If the internal interaction is supported but the reference class or normal choice is not, keep the pair as an open question. Select no pair when either strategy is not current, the mechanisms belong to unrelated bets, the exclusion is a straw man, or copying difficulty means only that two hard things are hard.

## Use explicit dispositions

Every strategy receives one disposition:

| Disposition | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Current | The mechanism operates now and passes every current-selection gate. |
| Not yet | A present precursor, causal path, missing condition, promotion test, and approved horizon are established. |
| Open question | A specific obtainable fact could materially change the mapping. |
| Insufficient evidence | The record cannot establish or refute the mechanism, and no material evidence-led question is available. |
| Rejected | The mechanism is contradicted or fails materiality, independence, explanatory value, sibling fit, present relevance, or speculation controls. |
| Not applicable | The mechanism's domain is structurally absent from the company bet. |

When nothing stands out, say nothing stands out.

Use that result only when no strategy clears the current-selection gates for any material company bet. One supported current strategy is not nothing.

## Freeze judgment before prose

The pre-prose audit must retain:

- the evidence cutoff, source registry, stable evidence IDs, attribution, dates, scope, and limitations
- observed facts, inferences with bridges, judgments with their basis, counterevidence, and material open questions
- the company bet or bets, rejected alternatives, scope decision, and a plain-world condition that would make the overall conclusion wrong
- one disposition for each of the 80 strategies
- full categorical reasoning for every positively supported or materially disputed strategy
- sibling checks, disposition reasons, and what evidence would change a live uncertainty
- the uncapped ordered current set
- zero or one pair record with reference class, normal choice, exclusion, interaction, and copying difficulty
- not-yet records with precursor, path, missing condition, horizon, and promotion evidence
- validation that names, evidence references, inferences, judgments, dispositions, and selected sets agree

Strategies that fail the evidence gate do not need empty prose fields. A compact disposition and reason are enough.

The writer receives the frozen judgment. It may not add, remove, swap, or reorder strategies. It renders the approved verdict and canonical meanings only.

## Test judgment before prose

Use unseen, non-holdout companies that cover one clear bet, several products under one bet, genuinely different bets, dramatic facts, live and stale historical mechanisms, generic category capabilities, sibling cases, valid and invalid pairs, credible and speculative not-yet claims, sparse evidence, and nothing-stands-out cases.

Every test pack keeps stable evidence IDs, source dates, attribution, source parity, and relevant contradictions. Do not reveal the intended answer through pack structure.

Test the structured judgment before any writer sees it. The judge and sole verifier cannot be the same draw.

Required perturbations include:

- reorder evidence and neutralize the dramatic fact
- mask company, founder, investor, and customer names while preserving mechanisms
- randomize the 80-label order
- separate historical from recent evidence
- change one sibling-discriminating fact
- remove pair reference-class evidence
- remove the only support for a selected strategy
- vary prose constraints, including no prose
- reduce the pack to sparse evidence

The judgment should change only when substantive evidence changes. Fail the method for unsupported current labels, omitted supported mechanisms, unresolved siblings, invented pairs, stale historical carryover, speculative not-yet claims, false abstention, failure to abstain, broken evidence entailment, or instability caused by names, order, vividness, or sentence shape.
