# Fresh test queue

The living list of fresh companies to run through Cold Start. One file, always current: statuses here are checked against the production `cards` table, not memory. Dated snapshots live in `docs/archive/qa/`. Theme since August 2026: biology as a creative medium.

## What earns a slot

[Future Society](https://arcaea.com/brands/) is the reference: biology creates an experience that previously could not exist, the science is clear in one sentence, and the finished object has enough taste to carry the idea into culture. A company earns a slot when it meets at least three of these tests:

- The technology changes what the consumer can experience, not only the supplier's cost.
- The scientific idea is clear in one sentence.
- There is a real object, sample, launch, or commercial partner to inspect.
- Design and storytelling matter to adoption.
- A hard business question remains for Investor Lens to answer.
